Lucidly

Master's thesis in Motion Design · 2025

Full project →

A 60-second commercial for the Lucidly app, built on a visual identity and voice-over already given, covering all of its features. The work moved from script and storyboard to style frames, where the stylistic choices were set. The final video works the colours of the app icon over shades of blue and purple, with 3D elements. Scenes built in After Effects and Cavalry.

Poster for Speed — Motor Racing from 1900 to 1950

A thesis tracing the origin of the graphic codes used to represent speed, through the visual communication of Italian motor racing in the first half of the twentieth century. Built on archival primary sources, original posters, photographs, documents, alongside bibliography, to contextualize their cultural and artistic impact. Chronological and thematic structure, typography drawn from the period, and throughout the red assigned to Italian racing cars in the 1930s, cut with flashes of light.

Roma 1975

Visual identity for the photographic exhibition Roma 1975, città, volti e storie nell'anno giubilare, for the Soprintendenza Speciale di Roma and the Drugstore Museum. The graphic line is built on a condensed sans-serif widely used in 1970s Italy. I handled promotional communication, posters and social, and internal graphics: information panels, captions, decorative artifacts. I also designed the exhibition catalogue, published by Scienze e Lettere, which collects the selected photographs and, through critical essays, reconstructs the historical, demoethnoanthropological, political and cultural context of 1970s Rome.
