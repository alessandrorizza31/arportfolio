Work About

I'm Alessandro a Visual and Motion Designer with two years of experience, specializing in 2D animation, branding and editorial projects. I also have a strong passion for Graphic design history and photography.

I work between exhibition design, branding and publishing: motion systems for museums and cultural institutions, visual identities, and the editorial design of exhibition catalogues.

Alongside project work I hold a research grant at Università Iuav di Venezia on data visualization for public programmes, and teach as a course assistant in communication design and motion graphics.
